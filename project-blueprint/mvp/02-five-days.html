The Five Days

The five days as a strip

Each day feeds the next. Friday is drawn differently because it is the only day whose outcome is not yours to produce.

The checklist

Written as outcomes, not tasks — each one either happened or it didn't. Nothing here is checked off yet; the week hasn't run.

Why Friday is a different kind of day

Monday through Thursday can be finished alone at a keyboard. Friday cannot. The whole week is built so that one person who did not write any of it can look at one screen and answer one question — and their answer, not yours, is the result. See Did It Work? for what counts as a pass.
